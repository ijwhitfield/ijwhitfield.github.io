Instructions

tab to switch between play and edit
move with wasd or arrow keys

edit:
 copy and paste levels from the clipboard
 scroll to zoom
 drag a line perpendicular to its length to move it
 shift+drag to make a new line as you move it
 drag a line into another line to remove it
 click a sector to change its color forward
 shift+click a sector to change its color backward
play:
 hit all green sectors to finish the level
 shift for direct movement
 space to reset
